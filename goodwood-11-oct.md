## Race by race: picks

### 13:47: Alderbrook Handicap (professional jump jockeys), Class 4, 0-80, 2m. 12 runners
**Forecast:** 11/4 Carthusian, 5/1 Kotari, 13/2 Klycot, 12/1 Solar Pass, 16/1 Aggagio (Poncho's price not found; he was 8/1 last year).
- **Pick 1: CARTHUSIAN (J Candlish), 11/4, win.** He's going for a hat-trick after winning his last two and prefers softer ground, which he gets today. The Racing Post Spotlight verdict (Richard Austen): "probably isn't the time to desert" him.
- **Pick 2: PONCHO (G & J Moore / Freddie Mitchell), each-way.** He **won this exact race last year off 69** and runs off **70** today, just 1lb higher. He was also **2nd in it two years ago**, and he has been lightly raced since. His Goodwood record over this trip is the best in the race, and the Moores do well here. Racing TV's Angus McNae tips him too.
- *I've left out Solar Pass (Betfred, 12/1).* It had been my earlier each-way pick, but Poncho's two good runs in this exact race make a stronger case than Solar Pass's connections alone.

### 14:22: Virgin Bet / British EBF Restricted Novice, 2yo, 6f
**Forecast:** 13/8 Brynmair Gold, 2/1 War Chest, 5/2 Cornichon, 12/1 Binfluencer.
- **Pick: BRYNMAIR GOLD (O Cole), 13/8.** He was beaten a neck on his Kempton debut. The Racing Post verdict and the Sussex Express both side with him. **Small stakes only.** His form comes from the all-weather, the price is short, and Betdaq's stats column suggests opposing him at about 2.76. **Danger: War Chest.**

### 14:57: Irish Stallion Farms EBF Nursery, 2yo, 1m1f11y
**Forecast:** 9/4 Zarak Crown, 9/2 Under Arrest, 5/1 Cloud Chorus, then Lots Road (about 7/1 with Millar).
- **Pick 1: LOTS ROAD, around 7/1.** He runs off a **low mark of 68**, and today is his first handicap and his first try at this longer trip. He looked green in his novice runs, so there's room to improve. Three separate sources pick him: the Racing Post verdict (Paul Smith), Ross Millar (Racing TV) and Betdaq.
- **Pick 2: ZARAK CROWN (R Fahey / James Doyle), 9/4.** He **won at Ayr over 1m on good to soft** last month, so the trip and ground are proven, and Doyle rides. The doubt is that he gives weight to the whole field on his handicap debut. That's why I prefer Lots Road at the bigger price.

### 15:32: Virgin Bet Daily Extra Places Handicap, Class 3, 6f. 14 runners
**Forecast:** 5/1 Deep Havana, 6/1 Midnight Thunder, 13/2 Bone Marra, Percy's Star, The Strikin Viking.
- **Pick 1: DEEP HAVANA (Wathnan / H Al-Jehani / James Doyle), 5/1.** He won at Thirsk on his first run for this stable, then disappointed as favourite in the Ayr Silver Cup. **Doyle has chosen him over stablemate The Strikin Viking**, which is a strong signal. The Racing Post Spotlight expects the two stablemates to fight it out, and Betfred tips him.
- **Pick 2: THE STRIKIN VIKING, 13/2, as a saver.** He's from the same yard and covers you if the yard is right but Doyle's choice is wrong.
- ⚠️ **Caveat:** I can't see the draw, and on soft ground the draw often decides 6f races at Goodwood. The Sussex preview also thinks Deep Havana prefers ground slightly less testing than today's.

### 16:07: Virgin Bet EBF Novice Stakes, 2yo, Class 3, 1m1f11y
**Forecast:** 5/4 Royal Acclamation, 5/2 Iron Curtain, 4/1 Donegan, 9/1 Bundaberg, 12/1 Wilcox Bridge.
- **Pick 1: IRON CURTAIN (R Beckett / Hector Crouch), 5/2.** He was 2nd on debut at Salisbury, and Beckett does well with this type of horse in the autumn. Ross Millar (Racing TV) and the Sussex Express both tip him. He's better value than the 5/4 favourite. (One early card listed his trainer as C Johnston, but the Racing TV and Millar pieces say Beckett and Crouch, so I've gone with that.)
- **Pick 2: BUNDABERG, 9/1, each-way.** He's the Racing Post Spotlight pick (Richard Austen): "the top four have shown plenty and BUNDABERG could come out on top".

### 16:42: Inkerman London Handicap, Class 2, 0-105, 1m. 13 runners, inside stalls
**Forecast:** 4/1 Wechaad, 5/1 Naval Light, Sword Maker, 6/1 Brian, Eternal Force, 7/1 Crazee Icon, 8/1 Cerulean Bay.
- **Pick 1: CERULEAN BAY, 8/1, each-way.** He **won this race 12 months ago** and runs off only **2lb higher**. He's the top-rated horse in the race and the Racing Post handicappers' nap, and Robbie Wilders includes him in his Punt Acca. It's a big price for a horse with proven course and distance form at this meeting.
- **Pick 2: WECHAAD (R Varian / Ray Dawson), 4/1.** He's a 3yo who should still improve, and the Spotlight says he "has caught the eye in all his races". His **4th at Goodwood in May** came after he was left with plenty to do, and the step up to 1m should suit.
- Also considered: Sword Maker (Charlie McCann's each-way nap, also up to 1m) and Crazee Icon (Balding, won 3 of her last 4, but now 9lb higher).

### 17:17: Virgin Bet A Good Bet Fillies' Handicap, Class 4, 3yo+ rated 66-85, 1m2f. 13 runners
**Forecast:** 4/1 Prosperity, 5/1 Room Fourteen, 11/2 Spirit Of Athene, 7/1 Withtearsinmyeyes, 8/1 Brighton Beach, 10/1 Winged One, Wisper.
- **Pick 1: ROOM FOURTEEN (J Fanshawe / Billy Loughnane), 5/1.** She's been "hard to fault since sent into handicaps in a hood". She **drops in grade and in trip**, and she has **already won at Goodwood**. The Racing Post Spotlight (Alistair Jones) picks her, and Betdaq's columnist calls her chance solid.
- **Pick 2: PROSPERITY (K Burke), 4/1.** She's unbeaten in three and going for four. She's the Betdaq stats nap, but the Sussex Express notes "this is harder". She's the main danger rather than my pick, because her mark has gone up after each win.
